Good to know.

Who can share an idea?

Anyone. We especially want to hear from young people aged 13–30 — the age range for Erasmus+ youth exchanges — but youth workers, teachers and parents are welcome too.

Do I need to have been on an Erasmus+ project before?

Not at all. Some of the best ideas come from people who have never taken part in one. Fresh eyes are exactly what we’re looking for.

Does taking part in a youth exchange cost money?

Erasmus+ youth exchanges are funded by EU grants that are meant to cover the main costs — typically travel (within set limits), accommodation, meals and activities. We’ll always tell you upfront if anything isn’t covered.

Will my idea definitely become a project?

We can’t promise every idea gets funded, but we promise every idea gets read. The topics many of you ask for are the ones we build our applications around.

What happens to my data?

Your answers are used only to plan GoActive Bucharest projects. We never sell or share them. Want your answers deleted? Email daniel.butnar@gmail.com and we’ll remove them. Full details are in our Privacy Policy.
