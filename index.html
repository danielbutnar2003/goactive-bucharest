01 Our mission

We keep one chair open at every table — for the young person who has never been invited to sit at one.

We call it The Open Chair. Before we choose a theme, a country or a single activity, we ask one question: who is missing from this room — and what would it take to bring them in?

Erasmus+ gives young people something rare: a real seat in Europe. Travel, accommodation and learning, funded so that money, a small hometown or a first passport are not the reasons someone stays at home. Our job is to make sure that seat reaches the people it was designed for.

And once they are in the room, we do the part that matters most. We hand over the microphone. Young people don’t just attend our projects — they shape the topics, the activities and the next destination.

02 What we stand for

Three things we don’t compromise on.

Inclusion, connection and a real voice. Everything we organise is built on these three.

01

Inclusion is the starting point

Not a quota and not a checkbox. We design for young people with fewer opportunities first — rural communities, tight budgets, disabilities, minority backgrounds, first-time travellers. When a project works for them, it works for everyone.

02

Erasmus+ is the bridge

A youth exchange puts young people from different countries under one roof — workshops, debates, shared meals, late-night conversations. Prejudice rarely survives a week of cooking dinner together. Friendships do.

03

A voice with consequences

Asking young people for their opinion is easy. Building the next project around it is the point. The ideas sent through this website become the themes, activities and destinations of what we apply for next.

03 Who it’s for

Built for the ones who usually get skipped.

If any of these sound like you, you’re exactly who we’re looking for.

  • 01

    You’ve never left Romania.

    Perfect. Your first trip abroad could be a youth exchange.

  • 02

    Money is tight.

    Erasmus+ grants exist precisely so that cost doesn’t decide who takes part.

  • 03

    You live far from a big city.

    Your postcode should never decide your opportunities.

  • 04

    You need extra support.

    The programme can fund additional support for participants. Tell us what you need.

  • 05

    Your English isn’t perfect.

    Nobody’s is. Youth exchanges run on curiosity, not grammar.

  • 06

    Nobody ever asked what you think.

    We’re asking now. Tell us →

05 How it works

From a sentence you type to a plane ticket.

This is how a young person’s idea becomes a real international project.

  1. 01

    You speak

    Share an idea on our platform — a theme, a place, an activity, even a frustration you want to solve.

  2. 02

    We listen

    We read every submission and look for the patterns: what young people in Bucharest and beyond really care about.

  3. 03

    We build

    With partner organisations across Europe, we turn the strongest ideas into an Erasmus+ project application.

  4. 04

    You go

    Once funded, we open the call — and make sure it reaches the young people who wouldn’t otherwise get the chance.

06 Our promise

We don’t speak for young people.

We pass the mic.

Being young and being heard shouldn’t be two different things. GoActive Bucharest exists to close that gap — one exchange, one idea, one voice at a time.
