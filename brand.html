Brand identity · Logo

The voice arrow.

A speech bubble that refuses to stay still. Our logo puts the whole mission into one mark: young people speaking up — and going places.

Voice Go Roots

01 The concept

Speak up. Go further.

  • The bubble — voice

    Every project starts with something someone says. The bubble is the platform we give young people to speak.

  • The arrow — go

    Pointing up and out: moving forward, crossing borders, taking the next step. The spirit of every Erasmus+ exchange.

  • The tail — roots

    The bubble’s tail points down and back, toward home. The arrow points the other way. You always leave from somewhere — for us, that’s Bucharest.

  • G

    The lettering — bold & open

    Custom wide, heavy letters whose rounded shapes echo the bubble. BUCHAREST is drawn light and spaced wide: our home base, with room to breathe.

02 Variants

One mark, every background.

Use the dark version on dark or saturated colours, the light version on light backgrounds, and the single-colour versions when only one colour is possible.

03 The icon

Recognisable at any size.

Use the icon alone for profile pictures, favicons, stickers and merch. It holds up all the way down to 16 pixels.

04 Clear space

Give it room.

Keep a clear space around the logo at least equal to x — the height of the letter O. Don’t use the horizontal logo narrower than 120 px on screen or 30 mm in print.
