01 The mission

Seen. Heard. Counted.

Some young people are loud. Many more are simply never seen — in their schools, in their towns, or in the rooms where decisions about their lives are made.

Vlad’s work is built around changing that. More than thirty Erasmus+ projects have shown him what happens when a young person is given a role, a stage and an audience that genuinely listens: confidence arrives first, and ambition follows close behind.

Visibility isn’t about being in the spotlight. It’s about being taken seriously — having your name on the project, your idea in the programme and your voice in the final result.

02 At GoActive

From idea to reality.

As project manager, Vlad turns the ideas young people send us into projects that can actually happen.

Plans that work

Shaping raw ideas into clear objectives, activities and timelines that a project team — and a funder — can believe in.

Partners in sync

Keeping organisations from different countries aligned, so that every group arrives prepared and nothing falls between the cracks.

Visibility by design

Building moments into every project where participants lead, present and share their work — so their voices travel further than the exchange.

Lessons from 30+ projects

Knowing from experience what makes a mobility unforgettable for participants — and what to avoid.

Every young person deserves to be seen.
