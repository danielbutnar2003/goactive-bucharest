Cookie Policy.

01What cookies are

Cookies are small text files a website stores on your phone or computer. Similar technologies, such as local storage, session storage and tracking pixels, can also store or read information on your device. Websites often use them to remember settings, measure visitors or show advertising.

02Cookies on this website

We built GoActive Bucharest to work without any of them. The website itself does not place cookies on your device, and it doesn’t use local storage, session storage or any other technology to store or read information on your device.

NameProviderPurposeDuration
None. This website sets no cookies.

03Third-party services

  • Fonts and images are stored on our own website, not loaded from Google or other external servers.
  • Forms are sent in the background to FormSubmit, which delivers them to our inbox. No cookies are placed on your device when you send a form.
  • Hosting: the site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which does not set cookies on our website.
  • Instagram and Facebook are plain links, not embedded content. Nothing from Meta loads on our pages. If you click through, you are on Meta’s websites, and their cookie policies apply there.

How we handle the data you send us is explained in our Privacy Policy.

04Why there’s no cookie banner

Under the EU ePrivacy rules, implemented in Romania by Law no. 506/2004, a website needs your consent before it stores or reads information on your device that isn’t strictly necessary. Because this website stores and reads nothing, there is nothing to ask your consent for — so we don’t interrupt you with a banner.

05Controlling cookies in your browser

You don’t need to do anything for our site. For other websites, every browser lets you view, block and delete cookies in its privacy settings — look for “Privacy”, “Cookies” or “Site data” in Chrome, Safari, Firefox, Edge or your phone’s browser.

06Changes & contact

If we ever add something that uses cookies, such as visitor statistics, we will update this policy first and ask for your consent before any non-essential cookie is set.
